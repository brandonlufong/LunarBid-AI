# LunarBid — User Guide

LunarBid helps freelancers and agencies turn a project brief into a personalized proposal
draft, so you spend less time starting from a blank page. This guide covers everything you
can do in the app today. It's available in **English and French**, with **light and dark**
themes (switch both from Settings or the account menu).

> **Free beta:** LunarBid is in free beta. Core features are live, usage limits apply, and
> **no card is needed**. More plans arrive later — your feedback shapes them.

---

## 1. Create your account
1. Open the app and choose **Get started** / **Sign up**.
2. Sign up with your **email and password**, or with **Google** or **GitHub** (when those
   buttons are shown).
3. **Confirm your email.** We send a verification link; click it to activate AI features.
   Didn't get it? Use **Resend** on the banner, or check spam.

**Signing in later:** use **Sign in**. Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password?**,
enter your email, and follow the reset link we send (it expires after 1 hour).

---

## 2. Your dashboard (Home)
After signing in you land on **Home**, which shows:
- A **setup checklist** built from your real state (confirm email → add your profile →
  create your first proposal).
- Your **usage** for the current period.
- **Recent proposals** with their status.
- A clear **New proposal** button — the main action.

Navigate with the sidebar (desktop) or the menu drawer (phone/tablet). Every screen has its
own URL, so reload and the browser's Back button work.

---

## 3. Write a proposal (the core feature)
Open **New proposal** / **Generate**. It's three guided steps:

1. **The job** — paste the job post or describe the project (you can **Try an example** to
   see how it works). The draft follows the **language of the job post**.
2. **Details (optional)** — client name, budget, and so on. If you've saved client profiles,
   you can start from a client to prefill these.
3. **Style** — choose a **tone** (formal, friendly, persuasive) and **length**. The tone
   defaults to your profile preference. The **usage cost is shown before you generate**.

Click **Generate**. LunarBid writes a first draft in seconds. If every AI provider is briefly
unavailable, you'll get a clearly-labelled template instead (and it isn't counted against your
quota).

> The draft is a **starting point**, not a finished proposal. Always review and personalize it.

### Analyze a job (optional)
Before or after generating, open **Analyze** to get an at-a-glance read of the job: key
requirements, suggested skills, likely budget range and tone — all **labelled as estimates**.
A profile-match score only appears once you've filled in your profile.

---

## 4. Review, edit and deliver the result
The result is shown as a **document** (readable, with a title and meta). From here you can:
- **Edit / Done** — make changes inline; you're warned about unsaved changes.
- **Regenerate** — produce a fresh draft.
- **Copy** — copy the text.
- **Export** — download as **PDF, Word, Markdown, or plain text**.
- **Share link / Stop sharing** — create a public link to the proposal (and revoke it anytime).
- **Send by email** — email the proposal to yourself or a client.

On phones these sit under a compact **More** menu.

---

## 5. History and outcomes
**History** lists your proposals (list on desktop, list→detail on phones). Open one to view
or edit it. Mark a proposal **won** or **lost** — this feeds your **Analytics** so your
numbers reflect real outcomes.

---

## 6. Clients *(Starter plan and above)*
Save **client profiles** (name, company, contact, preferred tone/style, notes). Starting a
proposal from a saved client prefills their details, so repeat work is faster.

---

## 7. Rate / bid calculator
The **Pricing** calculator gives a transparent, deterministic bid estimate from your inputs
(project type, hours, hourly rate, complexity, timeline, expected revisions). It shows a
**recommended figure with a conservative–premium range** and a breakdown of how it's built
(base labour → complexity → revisions → rush → platform-fee cover). Copy the figure to use it.

---

## 8. Analytics *(Pro plan and above)*
See your proposal **win rate**, totals and trends over time, based on the outcomes you record
in History. Charts load only on this screen.

---

## 9. Branding *(Pro plan and above)*
Add your **logo, brand colours, company name and website**. Your branding appears on the
**public share page** of your proposals, so shared links look like yours.

---

## 10. Settings
Settings is organised into:
- **Profile** — your role, skills, experience and bio. LunarBid only uses what you enter here;
  it never invents details. A fuller profile makes better drafts.
- **Preferences** — language (English/Français) and theme (light/dark).
- **Security** — change your password; sign out of other devices.
- **Your data** — export your data, or delete your account.

---

## 11. Plans, limits and billing
- **Free** during beta — generous enough to try everything, with **no card required**.
- Usage limits apply (a few proposals per day and a monthly allowance). You'll always see a
  **clear message** when you reach a limit, and when it resets.
- **Online payments aren't available yet.** Paid plans and the Agency plan are shown as
  **coming soon**; nothing will charge you.

---

## 12. Help, feedback and languages
- **Help & Support** (in the menu) has a contact form — the best way to reach us and to
  **send beta feedback**.
- Switch **language** (EN/FR) and **theme** (light/dark) anytime from the account menu or Settings.

---

## 13. Common messages
- **"Confirm your email"** — click the verification link before using AI features (Resend if needed).
- **"You've used your N proposals for today / this month"** — you hit a usage limit; it resets as stated.
- **"AI generation is temporarily paused"** — a brief maintenance pause; try again shortly.
- **"Online payments aren't available yet"** — expected during beta; you won't be charged.
